Copyright & DMCA Policy

How YT4MP3 respects intellectual property, handles takedown requests under 17 U.S.C. 512(c)(3), and processes counter-notifications.

YT4MP3 Media Technologies respects the intellectual property rights of creators, publishers, musicians and other rights holders, and expects every visitor using YT4MP3 to do the same. This Copyright and Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) Policy explains user responsibility on our site, how rights holders can submit a takedown notice, and how counter-notifications are handled.

Our Respect for Intellectual Property

Copyright protects original creative works—including video footage, musical compositions, sound recordings, lectures and scripts—from unauthorized copying or distribution. We support lawful, respectful use of online media and maintain this policy in accordance with the notice-and-takedown framework of 17 U.S.C. 512 and the rules set out in our terms of service.

When a rights holder raises a valid concern regarding a specific URL or link processed through our utility, we take the report seriously and evaluate appropriate action, such as blocking that source URL from being processed by our converter.

No Hosted Media Libraries

To clarify how our service operates, please note that YT4MP3 is an on-demand browser utility. It does not host, index, publish or store a searchable library of audio or video files. There are no public user channels, media feeds or stored catalogues on our servers.

The converter responds only when a visitor submits a specific public link for temporary cloud processing and delivers the resulting file directly to the visitor's browser. Because we do not operate a hosted media repository, identifying the exact source URL in your notice is essential so our team can locate and evaluate the reference.

User Responsibility for Rights and Permissions

Every user of YT4MP3 is solely responsible for verifying that they have the legal right to convert and save the material linked in their request. Acceptable examples include your own original uploads, works licensed under a compatible Creative Commons licence, verified public-domain recordings, or material for which you hold explicit permission from the copyright owner.

YT4MP3 does not support paid, rented, DRM-protected, live or private content, and users must comply with applicable copyright law and platform Terms of Service. For practical guidance on licences and ownership, see our about us page or our blog guides.

How to Submit a DMCA Notice

If you are a copyright owner, or an agent authorized to act on behalf of one, and you believe that material referenced or processed through YT4MP3 infringes your copyright, you may submit a written DMCA notification. Under 17 U.S.C. 512(c)(3), an effective notification must be a written communication that includes substantially all of the following elements:

  1. Signature: A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work: Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, or, if multiple works are covered by a single notice, a representative list of those works.
  3. Identification of the material and URL: Identification of the material claimed to be infringing or to be the subject of infringing activity, along with information reasonably sufficient to permit us to locate it, including the exact source URL(s).
  4. Contact information: Information reasonably sufficient to permit us to contact the complaining party, such as a physical address, telephone number and email address.
  5. Good-faith statement: A statement that the complaining party has a good-faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. Accuracy statement under penalty of perjury: A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that the complaining party is authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.

Where to Send Your Notice and Review Timing

Please send completed notices through the contact form or designated email listed on our contact us page, using the subject line "DMCA Notice" so your submission reaches the review queue properly.

Missing statutory elements may delay evaluation because we may need to request clarification. Once a complete notice is received, we aim to review notices promptly and take appropriate action where a valid claim is established, such as disabling processing for the identified URL(s). We do not make fixed response-time promises, as review depends on verification and completeness.

Counter-Notification Procedure

If access to a URL was disabled following a DMCA notice and you believe the action resulted from a mistake or misidentification, you may submit a written counter-notification via our contact us page with the subject line "DMCA Counter-Notice". Under 17 U.S.C. 512(g)(3), a valid counter-notification must include:

  • Signature: Your physical or electronic signature.
  • Identification of the material: Identification of the material or URL that was removed or disabled and the location where it appeared before removal or disabling.
  • Good-faith statement under penalty of perjury: A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification.
  • Contact details and consent to jurisdiction: Your name, address and telephone number, a statement consenting to the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court for the judicial district where your address is located (or any judicial district where YT4MP3 Media Technologies may be found if outside the United States), and agreement to accept service of process from the original complainant or their agent.

Upon receiving a complete counter-notification, we aim to review it promptly and follow applicable statutory notice procedures.

Repeat-Infringer Policy

In accordance with the DMCA and applicable law, YT4MP3 Media Technologies maintains a policy of restricting or terminating access, in appropriate circumstances, for users, IP ranges or recurring source patterns associated with repeat copyright infringement. We may also block specific URLs or channels that repeatedly generate valid takedown claims.

Abuse of the Notice Process

Submitting false or bad-faith copyright claims harms legitimate creators and disrupts lawful expression. Under 17 U.S.C. 512(f), any person who knowingly materially misrepresents that material or activity is infringing, or that it was removed or disabled by mistake or misidentification, may be liable for damages, including costs and attorneys' fees. Please verify ownership and any applicable licences before submitting a claim. Information on how we handle correspondence data appears in our privacy policy.
