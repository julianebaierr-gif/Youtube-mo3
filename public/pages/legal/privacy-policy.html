Welcome to YT4MP3.com. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data when you use our online media conversion tools. Privacy Commitment

At YT4MP3.com, we believe that converting media should be straightforward and private. We have designed our system so that you can convert YouTube videos to MP3 and MP4 formats without needing to create an account. No registration or personal sign-up is required to access our core features. You do not need to provide an email address, username, or password to use our tools. This anonymous approach ensures that your media conversion activities remain entirely confidential.

Information Not Collected

Because user privacy is central to our service, we deliberately avoid gathering sensitive data. We do not collect names, email addresses, credit card details, billing addresses, or any other form of personally identifiable information. Since financial transactions are not part of our platform, payment details are never requested, stored, or processed by our servers. You remain anonymous while interacting with our conversion tools, and we do not build user profiles based on personal identity.

Automatic Server Logs

To maintain platform security, prevent abuse, and ensure reliable server performance, our web infrastructure automatically records certain standard system details when you visit YT4MP3.com. These server logs may include:

  • Anonymized IP addresses
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system information
  • Timestamps of requests
  • Referring web pages

Please note that IP addresses recorded in our server logs are anonymized to prevent direct association with individual users. This data is used strictly for system administration, traffic analysis, and safeguarding our infrastructure against automated traffic spikes or service abuse.

Temporary Processing

When you input a URL to convert a video into MP3 or MP4 format, our system processes the media stream in temporary server memory (RAM). This processing occurs strictly on an as-needed basis to deliver the requested file directly to your device. We do not maintain a permanent archive of the media files you convert. Once the conversion process finishes and the file is delivered to your browser, the temporary data is purged automatically from our memory and storage caches. We do not store copies of converted audio or video files on our servers.

Cookies and Security

YT4MP3.com utilizes a secure HTTPS connection across all pages. This encryption ensures that data transmitted between your browser and our servers remains private and protected from interception by third parties. Regarding cookies, we do not employ tracking cookies or invasive analytics tools that follow your browsing habits across external websites. Any small data files placed on your device are strictly functional, serving to maintain session stability and proper website operation.
